# 2026-11-05

## Block playback bar (#58, #81, branch lib-block-bar)

- Plan `documentation/plans/v0.14 Block playback bar.md`, from the decisions in `documentation/design/58 playback bar/DESIGN.md`.
- New `src/playback/block-bar.ts`: a block's diagrams play together (`diagramGroup`: one thing at a time, selection, play all in turn, the sound for the whole block) and `blockBarFor` builds the one bar below them. `src/playback/bar-parts.ts`: what every bar shares (the static bar, icons, pressed buttons, value sliders, selects, `volumeOf`). `controls.ts` keeps the options, what a diagram plays and its redrawing; `attachPlayback` groups diagrams by `.mn-block`, and those of a progression or chart by its drawing (no bar of their own: the progression bar's sound is theirs, and a play on either stops the other).
- The hover bar is gone. A click on a diagram selects and plays it (again: stops), a note plays only itself, each `.mn-playable` is a button (Space, Enter, Escape), and a press on it never reaches the editor (live preview opened the block's source on a press on a diagram).
- Play all in turn: each diagram once, one beat (60 / BPM) between two, the selection following; loop starts again after the last. Ends on stop, when the block leaves the page, and on `destroy`.
- Every bar but the tab bar sits below its drawing, always shown, with no volume: `PlayOptions.volume` (now also a function, read at each play and each pass) is the volume. Strum, picking, progression and chart bars moved below and lost their volume too, for one look (chosen: consistency over the old place).
- "♩" fix: every slider shows its value after it ("90 BPM", "12 ms"; tabular digits, 7ch) and as `aria-valuetext`; the 6/8 tempo says "counts dotted quarters" in its tooltip and `aria-description`. The labels "Tempo (BPM)" and "Strum (ms between strings)" stay as they were (apps read them).
- #81: `cursor: pointer` on `.mn-block .mn-playable` and everything in it, and on every bar's buttons, selects and sliders; no `!important`. Without playback there is no `.mn-playable`: the arrow stays.
- The bar goes inside the `.mn-block`, after the element that holds the diagrams (a row, a captioned figure), so Publish never sees anything between a source `<pre>` and its block. In a fretboard exercise it goes between the fretboard and the tab.
- Specs first: `block-bar.spec.ts` (32; 29 failed on 0.13, the 3 that passed check what 0.13 already did). Old specs changed where the behavior changed on purpose: no Volume slider, the progression bar below, a press on a diagram kept from the page, the modes of a row played by a click on each.
- Checks: tsc, lint, 1017 tests, build; wiki check 164 files, 765 blocks, 763 ok, 0 errors, 2 skipped; `check --describe` of the wiki identical to 0.13.0. Browser (Chrome, the Publish bundle on a test page with the prototypes' content, a dense page, progression, strums, picking, chart, circle, exercise, both hands, an error block, a host rule `button { cursor: var(--cursor) }`): one bar per block below its drawing, hand cursor everywhere it plays, light and dark, 200% zoom, keyboard only (Tab to a diagram, Enter plays, focus ring), the sound switch over a row of six voicings, play all in turn, no console error. Fixed on the way: the focus and selection rings were cut by the block's scroll box (now drawn inside), a line-box gap above the bar of a single diagram, the first diagram not marked as selected at the start.
