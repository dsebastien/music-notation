## Bug fixes for 0.5.2 (unreleased)

- #29: shell voicings (root, third, seventh) are named with the fifth assumed (tonal's `assumePerfectFifth`): `3x44xx` Gmaj7, `x324xx` Cmaj7, `x313xx` Cm7.
- #30: 13th grips are named: tonal's `7add6` reads as 13 (`m7add6` as m13), a trailing `no5` is dropped (`G13no5` → G13): `020120` E13, `3x3455` G13.
- #31: a slash chord's bass is spelled as the matching chord tone, else with the accidentals of the chord's key: `x12010` C7/Bb.
- #32: scales without double accidentals: the altered scale uses superlocrian degrees when tonal's jazz spelling needs one (G# altered: G# A B C D E F#, R b2 b3 b4 b5 b6 b7). Other scales keep one letter per degree (G# harmonic minor's 7th stays F##): a first version gave every other double accidental its simplest name, which a review caught before the release.
- #28: keyboard scale fingering with `labels:` (labels on the keys, fingers in a row under them) and with a `range` (fingering from the first root inside it, every octave lit).
- #36: the playback bar's sound dropdown: its options take the page's colors (readable in dark themes).
- #39: playback sliders as tall as their thumb, the 3px track a centered gradient line: no thumb offset per host.
