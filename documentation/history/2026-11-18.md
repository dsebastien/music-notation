## The effects (#103): second pass after Sébastien's first listening (branch `feat/room-and-limiter`, not merged)

- Sébastien listened to the two listening pages: he heard little or no difference between the settings, and "weird audio issues" he could not describe. He asked for another pass before he listens again. Nothing below is a final default: he chooses the room's level and length, and the electric guitars' cabinet and drive, by ear.
- What the first pass had measured: six steady sines in an offline render (level, tail, peak), and on the real pages only which nodes were connected. The sound of the pages itself was never measured. This pass records what reaches the speakers of the listening pages in headless Chrome (a tap on every connection to a context's destination, `cap-lib.ts` in the harness) and renders those recordings offline through candidate chains (`tune.html`). Every number below names its signal.
- **The room was not audible.** Recorded steel strum of the page without a tab (sample player, GeneralUser GS): after the notes' end, the room at 0.16 was 25 to 30 dB under the notes and gone in 0.3 s; at 0.6 still about 20 dB under. A guitar's notes ring for seconds and hide a room of 0.7 s. Now: 1.2 s (`ROOM_SECONDS`), brighter (the tail's highs fall from 9 kHz to 2.4 kHz, in Hz, so the same at every sample rate; before, a per-point factor that closed to about 1.2 kHz), its sound under 180 Hz left out (`ROOM_LOW_CUT`), level 0.25 (`DEFAULT_REVERB`). The five early reflections are gone: at the level of the tail's own points they changed nothing. New option `room` (seconds, 0.2 to 3; `PlayEffects.room`, `PlayOptions.room`, `play: { room }` on a page), so the length can be chosen by ear; Sébastien decides whether it stays in the API.
- **The limiter worked on ordinary strums.** The recorded steel strum peaks at 0.83 and the accented six-string strum at 1.115; the threshold was -3 dB (0.71), so every strum was turned down a little, and the room with it (offline render of the recorded strum: gain swing 0.7 dB; of the accented one 2.8 dB). Now -1.5 dB, attack 2 ms, release 150 ms: the recorded steel strum comes out as it went in (peak 0.832 in and out, same level), the accented one at 0.903. A ceiling follows the limiter (`ceilingCurve`, a wave shaper: as it is under 0.9, never above 1): two accented strums at once (2.01 in) came out at 1.006 from the limiter alone.
- **The drive made a strum 10 to 12 dB louder** (offline render of the recorded clean and overdriven strums, and of the light synthesizer's; on the alphaTab page the overdriven strum went from -21.5 to -9.3 dB RMS at drive 0.5). Its level gain was a guess (`1 / (1 + 1.5 × drive)`), and the curve raises quiet sound by its slope (up to 25 times). Now the slope is 1 to 15 (`driveAmount`), the level after the curve is matched on a wave at the level of a strum (`driveLevel`, `DRIVE_REFERENCE` 0.2), and the deep bass is cut before the curve (120 Hz). Recorded on the pages: +0.9 dB (recorded overdrive, drive 0.5), +0.1 dB (light synthesizer's). The cabinet raised the level by 1.3 to 2.5 dB: it is followed by -2 dB (`CABINET_TRIM_DB`).
- **alphaTab's sound went out directly for about 20 ms at each start**, then was moved into the chain (whose limiter is 6 ms late): a jump at the first note of every play. `src/tab/room.ts` now gives alphaTab's context a `destination` of its own (a property on the object): alphaTab connects its new node to the chain itself at each start. Recorded on the alphaTab page, first play of a fresh page included: the node connects to the chain's gain, never to the speakers; with Dry it connects to the speakers. The move of a connected node stays as a fallback (a context that takes no property, or a play that began before the context was taken). The context is taken when the player says it is ready (`playerReady`), at each start and at `look`.
- **Making a room stalled the page**: 120 to 270 ms on the main thread at the first play of a context (a power per point). Now two products per point and the last three rooms kept: 32 ms for the first chain of a context in Chrome (impulse and convolver), 0 after.
- Room 0 now equals Dry on the recordings (same peak, same level, same envelope), for the sample player and for alphaTab.
- The listening pages (harness, and `tools/listen-fx` on `wip/listen-fx-103`): the Room level moves what sounds at once (the page finds the room's gain; the library applies a level at the next play), a Room length slider, levels up to 1. A looping strum takes Dry, the length and the amplifier at its next pass (checked on both pages by the connections each pass makes).

## The effects (#103): third pass, "there's noise when Dry is disabled, and it gets worse as the room level is increased"

- Sébastien listened to the second pass and heard a noise with the chain on, louder with more room. Still nothing final: he listens again and chooses by ear.
- **Where the noise is not.** (1) On the page without a tab, the recorded steel strum with the room at 0 equals the dry recording to -79 dB (recorded in headless Chrome): the limiter and the ceiling add nothing to an ordinary strum. (2) The live room equals a model made offline from the dry recording (the notes, plus the room made by hand): at 0.25, what is left with the level of each 10 ms taken out is 53 dB under the sound; at 0.5, 44 dB. (3) The same check in Sébastien's own Chrome (a tab opened through the Chrome extension, a probe that records what reaches the context's destination, his Bluetooth headphones as the output device, 48 kHz, output latency 216 ms): the same -44 dB at 0.5 on both sides, audio delivered regularly. So no glitch in the convolver, in the limiter or in the browser: the noise was in what the chain was asked to make.
- **The room itself (the first cause, by reasoning, not proven by ear yet).** Its impulse was noise as bright as the guitar (a single low-pass from 9 kHz), at full level from its first instant, with nothing in common between left and right. Each attack came back as a burst of noise, and on headphones the room was a cloud of noise apart from the guitar in the middle (also the hardest material for a Bluetooth codec). Measured on the recorded strums: the room's highs were as far under its mids as the dry guitar's (-35 dB on the steel strum), so not darker than the guitar at all. Now `ROOM_SHAPE`: three low-passes in a row from 4 kHz down to 800 Hz (the room's highs are 6.6 dB further under its mids than the dry steel strum's, 9 dB for the electrics), a rise of 50 ms, and half of each side in common (`width` 0.5; left and right alike at 0.71, before 0). It is louder in the mids for the same number (the energy of 1 is in a narrower band), so the default level went from 0.25 to 0.12. `roomImpulse(sampleRate, seconds, shape)` takes a shape, for the listening pages and for a host that tunes its own.
- **The limiter with a loud room (the second cause, raised by a review of this session, measured).** At the default level it does nothing to an ordinary strum (offline render of the recorded steel strum: left over at -130 dB). With a loud room it was pushed over its threshold on every strum and, being fast (2 ms, 150 ms), followed the wave: on the recorded steel strum with the new room at 0.3 and 0.6, 45 and 36 dB under the sound with the level of each 10 ms taken out; on the accented strum 42 dB with no room. Now 10 ms and 500 ms: 51 and 41 dB, and 46 dB on the accented strum. The ceiling takes the peaks it lets through: recorded on the page, the accented strum (1.115) comes out at 0.94, at 0.97 with the default room, at 0.99 with the room at 0.3.
- **A browser's compressor starts turned down** and comes back at its release time (offline: a strum far under the threshold was 2 dB down at once and 0.3 dB down after 50 ms with a release of 150 ms; with 500 ms, 2.7 dB down after 50 ms). So the limiter lets go in 20 ms for its first 0.3 s (`LIMITER_WAKE`), then takes its own release: 0.0 dB from the first note (offline), and the first play of a fresh page starts at the dry play's level (recorded).
- The `room` option (the length in seconds) stays in the API (Sébastien: "do what's best"): it costs nothing more, it has specs, and a host can ask for a hall.
- The listening pages: the room's Level, Length, Tone, Width and Soft start all move what sounds at once (the page gives the library's convolver a room made with the library's own `roomImpulse`), two buttons (The room of this morning, The new room) to compare, and a first question (Level 0 with Dry off: a noise or not?) that tells the room from the limiter.
- Final build recorded on both pages: the room at 0 equals Dry (steel 0.832, overdrive 0.418, alphaTab 0.642, the tab 0.757, same levels and envelopes); the amplifier with cabinet and drive 0.5 on the recorded overdrive is 0.5 dB up.
