## 0.4: voicings (music-notation#18)

- Spec agreed with Sébastien: the voicing generator first (library 0.4, Music Practice 0.6 #54); tabs and scores move to library 0.5 / plugin 0.7 (plan renamed).
- `generateVoicings` (main entry, no data): depth-first over each string's chord tones within the hand's span, then checks (required tones, bass, no inner mutes, at least 4 strings, no doubling on 3 strings while a tone is missing), fingers (`fingerVoicing`: one per note, else an index barre, then a barre on the highest fret) and a difficulty score. Shapes (`voicingShape`): power chord, triad, open, shell, then CAGED by sliding the voicing down to the open C A G E D chord of the same quality.
- Ranking tuned on the open chords (C A G E D Am Em Dm Cmaj7 B7 first), F's E-shape barre first, B♭'s A-shape high; open strings far below the frets and reaches across strings cost more, a muted high string more than a muted low one.
- `voicings:` blocks (`all` or a number, `area`, `root-string`, `strings`, `inversion`, `stretch`, `open`, `gaps`), titled with the chord and its shape. 210 tests. Checked in Obsidian through the plugin's dev build (rows, filters, ukulele, horizontal, error): screenshots in the session scratchpad.
- Adversarial review (separate agent): 3 high, 4 medium, 4 low, all addressed. Fingering is now a search (any finger barres adjacent strings on one fret, only the index lies over higher frets; one finger, one fret), which brings back C9 x32333, D9 x54555, C6 x35555, C13 x32335 and fixes one finger on two frets; re-entrant tunings default to any inversion (ukulele F 2010, G 0232 first); search pruning (a muted string closes the voicing without gaps; missing tones and strings), one tonal lookup per chord, span capped at 6, a cache of the last 32 lists, and 7–8 string blocks limited to one widening filter (`inversion: any` with six strings or fewer); ranking: a fixed list of the usual open chords first (−3), E-/A-shape and small barres cost 1, open strings ringing inside a shape up the neck cost more, so Bm x24432, Cm x35543, B x24442, Bb x13331 rank in the top two; shapes named from fret 12 (octave slide), 3-string R 3 7 voicings are shells; `voicings:` refuses `position`, `base`, `notes`, `fingering`, frets in the shorthand and unknown instruments, uses `title` as the prefix, reads `area: 0-3` from the nut, and says which filter emptied the list; `alt` reads as `7alt`. 222 tests.
