## Full screen for tabs and scores (#113)

- Asked by Sébastien after a review of web tab players: in full screen the score takes the whole width of an ultrawide screen, on several lines, and scrolls.
- `src/tab/full-screen.ts` (`fullScreenControl`, no alphaTab in it: `follow(on)` is the caller's) and its spec; wired in `renderTab` (`fullScreen`, default true); icons `full` and `exit` in the bar parts; styles `mn-fullscreen`.
- The score follows the music only in full screen: `scrollElement` is the block's surface from the start, `scrollMode` `OffScreen` there and `Off` elsewhere. #89 holds: out of full screen nothing scrolls.
- Checked in Chrome (a local page with the built publish and tab bundles, a 64-bar tab in a 700 px column, viewport 1462 x 726). The click was automated, so the browser refused its own full screen and the block covered the window (the fallback):
    - 2 bars a line in the page, 5 in full screen; the score's area scrolled (1,768 px of music in 664 px), the bar at the bottom, the page's background taken.
    - Playing from bar 20: the area scrolled to the next line by itself (scrollTop 624) and the page stayed where it was.
    - A wheel scroll stopped the following: the area stayed where the wheel left it for 7 s of playback.
    - Escape left it: the width, the focus (on the button) and the background came back.
    - Found there: leaving moved the page from 1,513 px to 5,665 px. Cause: the block is fixed in full screen, so the page below moved up, and the browser's scroll anchoring followed that text when the block came back and grew. Fixed with a placeholder of the block's height and a kept `min-height` until the score is drawn again; checked: the page at 4,461 px before, in, just after and 4 s after.
- Not checked: the browser's own full screen (it needs a real click), Obsidian (a popout window, the plugin's dev build), a phone, a 3,440 px or 5,120 px wide screen, the sound.
- Seen during the check, not changed: on a plain web page the tab bar is not sticky at the bottom of the screen. `.mn-block` has `overflow: auto`, which makes the block the bar's scroll container.
- One spec fails on Windows, before and after this change: `src/cli/check.spec.ts`, "files, folders (their Markdown files) and globs", expects forward slashes in paths and gets backslashes.
- `bun run tsc`, `lint` and `build` pass; `bun run test`: 1,220 pass, 1 fail (the Windows one above).

## Full screen: tabs drawn too wide with alphaTab's workers (same day)

- Found on a second local page that runs alphaTab in workers (a script URL of the same origin, as Music Practice does): on the branch, a 5-bar tab in a 657 px column was drawn 1,202 px wide, on one line. On `main` it was 657 px. The first check used the publish bundle, which draws on the main thread, and didn't show it.
- Cause: `scrollElement: surface` among the settings alphaTab starts with. Those go to its workers. Now set on `api.settings.player` right after the API is made, never sent. Spec: the start settings hold no element.
- Checked in Chrome with workers: 657 px in the column; in full screen 5 bars a line, the area followed the music (scrollTop 602) and the page stayed (4,109 px before, in and after); back at 657 px after Escape.
- `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on the full screen scroll area: the score was drawn 8 px wider than the area once its scroll bar showed.
- Sébastien tried full screen on the first page with a real click: it worked.
