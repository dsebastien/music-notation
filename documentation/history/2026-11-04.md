## Diagram titles that wrap (#74) and touch tap targets (#73 item 5), branch lib-size

- #74: a long title (the #72 names, a host's "9. " prefix, wiki titles such as "G shape, sounds Gb (F#)") widened a chord diagram or a fretboard; a host that draws them at a fixed width (Music Practice's voicing grid) scaled the whole drawing down. Now the title wraps, never widens (rule "Diagram titles"). First tried: a smaller font then `textLength` on one line, to keep the height; rejected after measuring the wiki: 108 of 291 blocks' titles would have been squeezed, "Mandolin G chop chord, G/D with D in the bass (G D A E tuning)" into 98 units. Wrapping instead: one title squeezed (that one, on its third line). `titleWidth` (8 for a space) broke "F#m7b5, Drop 3 (strings 2-4, 6)" into three lines inside "Drop 3"; `titleLineWidth` estimates closer and breaks after commas and before parentheses first: "F#m7b5, Drop 3" / "(strings 2-4, 6)".
- Rows: every chord diagram and fretboard of a block gets the room of its longest title (`alignTitleLines`), so nuts line up in `voicings:`, `---` rows, `positions: all` and chart diagram rows. Exported for apps (Music Practice #87): `alignTitleLines`, `titleLines`, `titleLineWidth`, `MAX_TITLE_LINES`, `TITLE_LINE`.
- Wiki, drawn with 0.11.0 and with the branch (291 chord and fretboard blocks): 218 diagrams are narrower (they had been widened by their title), 203 have two or three title lines or the room of them, 1 is squeezed. `check --describe` is identical to 0.11.0 (it reads the title's text, which wrapping keeps).
- #73 item 5: on a coarse pointer, chord diagrams and fretboards are drawn a third larger (`zoom: 1.34`), so a string's lane (18 units on chord diagrams, 20 on fretboards) is at least 24 px. CSS only (no inline style: a strict CSP on a Publish site would block style attributes). #15 (a size option) is not needed for this: the hit areas already span the whole lane, and only the drawn size was too small on touch screens.
- Specs: the title rules (one line, wrap, squeeze, row alignment, exports) and the coarse-pointer rule, each failing before; "makes room for a long title" changed on purpose (the diagram keeps its width). Checks: tsc, lint, 896 tests, build, wiki check (765 blocks, 763 ok, 0 errors, 2 skipped).
- Not checked here: the real text widths in Obsidian (the estimate decides the line breaks; a line wider than estimated would be clipped at the diagram's edge), and the zoom on a phone (Obsidian mobile, Safari, Firefox 126+ support `zoom`).
