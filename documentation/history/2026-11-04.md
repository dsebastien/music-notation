## Chart chords that last more or less than a bar (#75, branch lib-chartbars)

- Engine: `ProgressionBar.beats` (written beats; a bar when absent; the last chord fills its bar). `progressionEvents` builds a beat timeline: each beat strums the chord sounding then, `bar` strikes at the bar's start and each chord change, an arpeggio starts again on a new chord, a pattern slot plays its beat's chord. A pass's `strums` are one per chord (to light it), its `bars` one per bar. `playProgression` takes `{ chord, pitches?, beats? }`, calls `onBar` per bar (the chord sounding) and the new `onChord` per chord. Without `beats`, every output is as before (the old specs pass unchanged).
- Charts: a lyrics line with a bar line `|` is measured (`lineBeats`): chords between two bar lines share the bar, by dots (`[G5]. . [D5]. [A5].`, the Music wiki's riff notation) or slashes (`[C] / / [G]`) when they add up to the bar's counted beats, else evenly; an empty bar holds the chord before it (across lines); an N.C.-only bar is silent. Lines without `|` keep one chord a bar. Marks carry `data-mn-beats`; the drawing is unchanged. `chartChords(song, { transpose })` exports the same reading for apps (Music Practice #69/#89: use it instead of reading chart chords one a bar).
- Rejected: a duration inside the brackets (`[G*2]`): other ChordPro tools show it as a broken chord. A repeated chord (`[G] [G]`) already plays two bars.
- `expectLinear` moved to `src/test-setup.ts`, shared by the converter's and the chart's linear-time specs.
- Wiki: 41 of the 176 `chords` blocks write bar lines (12-bar blues, riffs, ii-V grids) and now play with those lengths (for example Power Chords' riff `| [G5]. . [D5]. [A5]. |`: 2 + 1 + 1 beats; Seventh Chords' Gmaj7 three bars). `check --describe` is identical to the published 0.11.0 (4772 lines; it doesn't describe chord lengths).
