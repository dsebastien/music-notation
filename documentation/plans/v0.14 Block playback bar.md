# v0.14: block playback bar

Goal: the playback UI decided on #58 (`documentation/design/58 playback bar/DESIGN.md`, section "Decisions"), with #81 (hand cursor) and the "♩" fix, then the Music Practice dependency bump.

## One bar per block, below it (#58)

- Every block with diagrams that play (`svg[data-mn-pitches]`: chord, keyboard, fretboard, circle; rows, `---`, `voicings:`, `positions: all`, `fingering: auto both`) gets one bar, a single diagram too. The hover bar inside each diagram is removed: nothing covers a diagram.
- Where: inside the `.mn-block`, after the element that holds its diagrams (the row, the figure with its caption), so it sits below them. In a fretboard exercise it goes between the fretboard and the tab (the tab keeps its own player). It never goes between the block's source `<pre>` and the `.mn-block` on Publish: the bundle redraws a block when something comes between them.
- The bar (`.mn-play.mn-play-static.mn-block-bar`, role group): play / stop (the selected diagram), play all in turn (only with two diagrams or more), the selected diagram's name (only with two or more), loop, the speed (BPM for a scale or a list, strum for a chord, only when the selected diagram plays several notes; it changes with the selection), the sound switch (guitar, piano; choosing the other instrument redraws every diagram of the block for it, as today's bar does for one).
- No volume on the bar: the app's `volume` option sets it (Music Practice Settings → Playback; Publish `window.musicNotationOptions.play.volume`). New: `volume` may also be a function read at each play, so an app's setting applies at once without drawing the blocks again.
- Selection: the first diagram is selected at the start. A click on a diagram selects it (outline, the bar's name) and plays it at once; a click on the diagram that plays stops it. A click on a dot, a key or a hit area selects the diagram and plays that note only. Each diagram is focusable (Tab), a button named "Play <name>" by a hidden text (`aria-labelledby`, not `aria-label`: Obsidian shows a tooltip for any element with one); Space or Enter plays or stops it; Escape stops what the block plays, a progression's bar included. The focus ring and the selection are outlines drawn inside the edge, over the drawing (kept in forced-colors mode).
- Play all in turn: each diagram once, from the first, at the bar's speed, with a pause of one beat (60 / BPM) between diagrams; the selection follows the diagram that plays. With loop on, it starts over after the last; stop ends it. Loop with play repeats the selected diagram, as today.
- One thing plays per block: a new play (a click, the bar, a note) stops what the block played.
- Live preview: a press on a diagram or on the bar is the block's (no editor click that opens the source).

## The other bars move below and lose volume too

- Strumming and picking patterns: their bar (play, loop, count-in, click, tempo, sound) moves below the pattern and is always shown (it floated over the pattern on hover). Progressions and ChordPro charts: below the chords (they were above). None has a volume slider: the app's `volume` sets it.
- Progressions and charts with chord diagrams: the progression bar is the block's one bar (no second bar). A click on a diagram plays it at once, Space or Enter on a focused one; dots play their note. The progression bar's sound switch also sets the sound of the diagrams; they are not redrawn for the other instrument in a progression (a progression cell holds a guitar grip or a piano voicing, chosen by the block).
- The tab and score bar (alphaTab) keeps its look until #82.

## The speed label ("♩" fix)

- The "♩" and "≋" glyphs before the sliders rendered tiny and below the bar's center line. Every bar (block, strum, picking, progression, chart) shows the value after the slider instead: "90 BPM", "12 ms" (tabular digits, fixed width, so the bar doesn't move while you drag). The value sits in `.mn-play-value`. The slider keeps its accessible name ("Tempo (BPM)", "Strum (ms between strings)": apps read them) and gets `aria-valuetext` with the value and unit. Compound time: "60 BPM"; the tooltip and `aria-description` say it counts dotted quarters.

## Hand cursor (#81)

- `cursor: pointer` on everything clickable: a playable diagram (all of it plays), its dots, keys and hit areas, every bar button, select and slider. Every selector has a class, so it outranks host element rules such as Obsidian's `button { cursor: var(--cursor) }`, and each comes again under `.mn-block` for hosts with class rules; no `!important`. Apps that call `attachPlayback` on their own DOM keep the hand.
- Without playback (`play` off) there is no `.mn-playable`: diagrams and notes keep the default arrow.

## API and compatibility

- `PlayOptions` keeps every field; `volume` also takes a function. `attachPlayback`, `TimedBar` (`element` is still the `.mn-play`, `add` still fills `.mn-play-app`) are unchanged.
- Changed for pages and apps that style or script the bars (breaking for them, documented in the CHANGELOG): the diagram bar is one per block, below it, not inside `.mn-playable`; no volume slider in any bar except the tab bar; strum, picking and progression bars are below their drawing and always shown; the value text is in `.mn-play-value` (the glyphs are gone; `.mn-play-label` stays for the tab bar); the strum and picking wrapper is `.mn-timed` (it was `.mn-playable`, which now means a playable diagram); `mn-play-open` (tap to show) is gone for diagrams and patterns; a diagram's `.mn-playable` wrapper is a focusable button.
