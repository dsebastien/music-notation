# v0.3: tabs and scores

Goal: Music Practice 0.4 (issues #15, #19, #20, #31 there).

- `tab` / `score` blocks: alphaTex through alphaTab (MPL-2.0, unmodified dependency), own entry point; or `file: [[Song.gp]]` (the host resolves the file).
- Playback: play, speed, loop, metronome; soundfont (sf3) and Bravura font bundled by the plugin (no network); Publish loads them from jsDelivr.
- Import: Guitar Pro 3–8, MusicXML, Capella through alphaTab's importer and AlphaTexExporter; text tabs → alphaTex (own parser); chords over lyrics → ChordPro.
- Export: Guitar Pro 7, MIDI, print.
- Play the light blocks: strum or play `chord` blocks, play scales and chords from `keyboard` / `fretboard` (click a key or dot for one note), tempo and loop; same soundfont as tabs, offline (Music Practice issue "Play chords, scales and notes from notation blocks").
- Publish: lazy-load the alphaTab entry only on pages with tab blocks.
