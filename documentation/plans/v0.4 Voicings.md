# v0.4: voicings

Goal: every playable way to play a chord (#18), for Music Practice 0.6 (issue #54 there: all the ways to play a chord). Tabs and scores move to 0.5. Triads on string sets (#19) and chord transitions (#21) build on it.

## Generator

- `generateVoicings(symbol, { tuning, area, bassString, strings, inversion, maxSpan, allowOpen, minStrings, gaps, maxFret })`: every fret combination within reach that plays the chord's tones (root required, fifth optional), on any tuning up to 8 strings; ranked by ease; each with fingers, base fret, bass string, inversion, shape name, difficulty and reasons.
- `fingerVoicing(frets)`: fingers for any voicing (index barre when four fingers aren't enough); `voicingShape(frets, symbol, tuning)`: Open, E-/A-shape barre, CAGED C/A/G/E/D, Power chord, Triad, Shell, Other barre.
- Main entry: no data, a few milliseconds per chord.

## Blocks

- `voicings: all` (at most 32) or a number, in a row; `area: 5-9` or `open`, `root-string: 5`, `strings: 1-4` (1 = the highest string), `inversion: 0..3|any`, `stretch: yes`, `open: no`, `gaps: yes`. Titles: the chord and its shape. `voicing: N` keeps reading the database.
