## Triads (#19) and labels (#26)

- `triadVoicings(chord, { strings, inversion, tuning })`: close triads (three adjacent strings, one chord tone each) on a string set, up the neck, with their inversion and top note.
- ```chord `triads: 1-3` (a chord: a row of its triads; a key, `G major`: its seven triads), `inversion:`; `labels: intervals | notes` on guitar diagrams (R, 3, 5 in the dots).
- Fretboards: `strings: 1-3` shows those strings only.

## Transitions (#21)

- `transition(fromFrets, toChord, { tuning })`: the target's voicings ranked by movement: anchor fingers (same string and fret), guide fingers (same string, slide), fret jump, then ease; tips in plain words.
- `smoothProgression(chords, { tuning })`: the voicing sequence with the least movement overall (ease and movement traded off).
- ```chord `from: A x02220` + `to: D` (the best three, anchors and guides marked); `progression: G D Em C`.

## Playback of the light blocks (#23)

- A control bar at the bottom of chord, keyboard and fretboard diagrams, shown on hover (tap on touch screens): play / stop, loop, speed when several notes play (BPM, strum speed), volume, sound (guitar, piano).
- Chords strum low to high then ring; scales up and down; a click on a dot or key plays it.
- A light Web Audio synthesizer (plucked string, simple piano); apps can pass a soundfont player (the tab entry's) for realistic sound. `play` render option with defaults.

## Tabs and scores (#24)

- `@dsebastien/music-notation/tab`: `tab / `score in alphaTex through alphaTab (MPL-2.0, unmodified); `staves: tab | score | both`; `file:` (the host supplies the bytes); player (play, speed, loop, metronome, count-in); theme colors.
- Assets from the host: script for workers (a blob URL works in Obsidian), the Bravura font (data URL), the soundfont (bytes). Publish: its own bundle, only on pages with tab blocks, assets from jsDelivr.

## Import and export (#25)
