# v0.19 Formula captions and accessibility (#52, #14)

## Goal

- #52: a line under a diagram that says what it is: the chord's notes, its formula, its other symbols and its quality in words; a scale's notes, intervals and steps.
- #14: diagrams that a screen reader can describe, and a root mark that does not rest on color alone.

## Decisions (Sébastien, 2026-11-10)

1. The root mark as a shape is a setting, off by default: render option `rootMark: 'color' | 'shape'` (default `color`) and a block key `root-mark: color | shape`; a block's line wins. Music Practice adds a setting "Root marks: Color / Color and shape" after the release. The playground gets a checkbox.
2. Shape: the root's dots on chord diagrams and fretboards are rounded squares, the finger or label still inside; an open string's (and a capo string's) root ring is a square ring; a keyboard's root key keeps its color and gets a small square on the key. Passing-tone roots too.
3. Always on, no setting: a description (`<desc>`) of every diagram, the contrast of the colors, and charts read in order. They change what a screen reader reads, never the look (except colors that fail contrast).
4. `caption: formula` on chord, keyboard and fretboard blocks; `caption: fingering, formula` writes both, fingering first; `caption: none` as before.

## Rules

- `root-mark:` (any case; `colour` too) is read for the whole block (`chord`, `keyboard`, `fretboard`, `progression`) and taken out of it before the diagrams read their keys; any other value is an error: `root-mark: write color or shape`. With shape the block carries `data-mn-root-mark="shape"`, and a diagram redrawn for the other instrument keeps the shape. `withRootMark(mark, draw)` (exported) draws under a mark, for apps that call the renderers themselves (drills). Without the option or the key, output is unchanged.
- A root square is a `<rect class="mn-dot mn-root mn-root-shape">` with the circle's data attributes (`data-midi`, `data-mn-string`, …), about the circle's area (sides 1.8 times the radius), corners a quarter of the radius. A keyboard's root key gets `<rect class="mn-root-mark">` (7 units, centered across the key, above its label, `pointer-events: none`), after the key's label so the label's sibling selectors still work.
- Descriptions: `<desc class="mn-desc">` in the diagram's `<defs>`, pointed at by `aria-describedby` (never `aria-label` on SVG). Guitar chords: the fingering in words (as `caption: fingering` writes it), then the roots' strings. Fretboards: the notes on each string, low string first, with their frets and the root. Keyboards: the lit keys low to high, with octaves, the root and fingers. Note names follow the naming.
- Contrast (WCAG 2.2: 3:1 for marks, 4.5:1 for text): checked for the fallback colors on white and on a dark page (#1e1e1e); colors that fail are changed (see the history).
- Charts: the chord above a lyric syllable is read before it (DOM order chord then lyric).
- `caption: formula`: chord: "Am7 (A minor seventh): A C E G. Formula 1 b3 5 b7. Also written Amin7, Ami7, A-7." (the symbol drawn, tonal's quality words, the notes, the intervals with 1 for the root and `b`/`#`, up to 5 other symbols tonal knows, without `^` spellings). Scale: "A minor pentatonic: A C D E G. Formula 1 b3 4 5 b7. Steps W+H W W W+H W." (H a half step, W a whole step, W+H three half steps, 2W four). A slash chord adds "G in the bass". Names in the block's naming. Without a chord or scale the library knows (a list of notes, an unknown symbol): `caption: formula needs a chord or a scale the library knows: add chord: or scale:` (chord blocks: a symbol). `caption: fingering` on a keyboard or fretboard block is an error saying it is for chord blocks.
- HTML: formula alone is `figcaption.mn-formula`; both are one `figcaption.mn-captions` holding `span.mn-fingering` and `span.mn-formula`. The fingering caption alone is unchanged (`figcaption.mn-fingering`). The formula stays when the play bar redraws the diagram for the other instrument (same notes); the fingering hides as before.

## Build

1. Specs first: root-mark key and option (shape elements, errors, row-wide, redraw, unchanged output without it), descriptions for the three diagrams (and naming), formula captions (chords, piano, keyboards, fretboards, scales, slash chords, naming, errors, both captions, rows).
2. `src/render/root-mark.ts` (mark state, `withRootMark`, `markShape`); guitar, fretboard and keyboard renderers; `renderBlock` reads the key; `redrawFor` keeps it.
3. `describeDiagram` in `dom.ts`; descriptions in the three renderers.
4. `src/theory/formula.ts` (`chordFormula`, `scaleFormula`); `captionOf` reads a list; captions on keyboard and fretboard blocks.
5. CSS: square marks, the key marker, contrast fixes.
6. Docs (`docs/blocks.md`, examples), playground checkbox and a formula example, README API, Business Rules, history.
