This page has a tab, so alphaTab plays everything: the diagrams and the tab. This is how Music Practice sounds in Obsidian, and a web page with tabs.

  1. Turn Loop on in the bar of a strumming pattern and press Play.
  2. First, put Level at 0 (Dry not ticked): the room is off and only the limiter is left. Is there a noise? Tell me yes or no: it tells me which part makes it.
  3. Press The room of this morning, then The new room, while it plays: is the noise gone, or less?
  4. Move Level, Length, Tone and Width while it plays, and try Soft start off. If a noise is left, find what removes it. Tell me the numbers you like (they are at the bottom of the bar).
  5. Dry is the sound of today (no room, no limiter, no amplifier), at the next pass of the loop.
  6. On the clean and overdriven guitars, try Cabinet and Drive (next pass). Tell me what must become the default for each guitar, if anything.
  7. The loud strum at the end of each part is the limiter's case: it must not crackle or pump.
