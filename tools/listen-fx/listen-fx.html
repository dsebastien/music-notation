Effects: listen (pages without a tab)

This page has no tab, so the recorded instruments play through the library's sample player, and the light synthesizer through its own code. This is how a web page without tabs sounds.

  1. Play a block with the yellow bar as it is: that is the proposed default (a light room, and a limiter).
  2. Tick Dry, press Play again: that is the sound of today.
  3. Move Room and press Play again, until the room is right. Tell me the number.
  4. On the clean and overdriven guitars, try Cabinet and Drive. Tell me what must become the default for each guitar, if anything.
